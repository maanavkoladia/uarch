General guidance on the dcache structure:
There is 1 dcache top that instantiates 4 dcache blocks and 1 MIO block.
The MIO block itself has no further hierarchy, there is jsut one file.
The Dcache block each intantiate a dcache bank, vcache, and an eviction buffers.
The Dcache banks have 8 direct mapped cache lines, the vcache has 4 fully associative cache lines.

to get data from the victim to the dcache and vice versa ther are swap buffers.

The vcache uses eviction buffers for evicting data to main mem if the line is dirty.

the dcache and vcache both uses ram cells and the eviction buffers are just registers.
the dcache and vcache both use a phased clock to write into the ram cells from memory.
